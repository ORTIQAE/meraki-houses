Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 September 2026 · Applies to enquiries about Olive House and Opuntia House

This page explains what happens to the personal data you share with us when you enquire about the Meraki Houses project — for example by filling in a form on Facebook or Instagram, writing to us by email or direct message, or asking for the project dossier. We keep it short and plain, as required by Articles 13 and 14 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Graziano Mascheri, owner of the Meraki Houses project in Kampi, Fournoi Korseon, Greece.

Contact for anything related to your data: g.mascheri@gmail.com

2. What data we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, email address, phone number.
  • Your answers to the enquiry form: which house you are interested in, your indicative budget, your timing, and how you prefer to be contacted.
  • Correspondence: what you write to us afterwards by email, WhatsApp, phone or direct message.
  • Technical data collected by Meta Platforms when you interact with our advertisements (see section 6). We do not receive your browsing data; we only receive what you type in the form.

3. Why we use it, and on what legal basis

  • To answer your enquiry and send you the project dossier (plans, prices, buying process) — steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • To follow up on your enquiry, arrange a call or a visit, and keep you informed about the project as it is built — our legitimate interest in selling the houses to people who asked about them, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. You can object at any time (section 7).
  • To comply with the law, for example anti-money-laundering checks if a purchase goes ahead — Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.

We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling, and we never sell it.

4. Who sees your data

  • Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd (Facebook, Instagram), which operates the enquiry form and delivers our advertisements. Meta acts as an independent controller for the data it collects on its platforms; its privacy policy is at facebook.com/privacy/policy.
  • ORTIQAE (Italy), the agency that manages our advertising and enquiry handling on our behalf, acting as our processor under a data-processing agreement.
  • Professional advisers — notary, lawyer, architect (WIERMAN.studio) — only if and when a purchase moves forward, and only what they need.
  • Email, messaging and storage providers we use to communicate and keep records (for example Google Workspace, WhatsApp).

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where that happens, transfers rely on the European Commission's adequacy decisions or on Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a purchase: 24 months from your last contact with us, then deleted.
  • If you buy a house: for the duration of the contract and as long as tax and legal obligations require (up to 10 years).
  • If you ask us to delete your data earlier, we will, unless we are legally required to keep it.

6. Advertising on Facebook and Instagram

Our advertisements run under Meta's "Housing" special ad category, which means Meta does not let us target them by age, gender, or detailed personal characteristics. When you open one of our forms, Meta pre-fills your contact details from your profile; you can edit them before sending. Meta may also show you our advertisements again based on your interaction with our page or videos, under Meta's own terms, which you control from your Facebook and Instagram ad settings.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can, at any time and free of charge:

  • ask what data we hold about you and receive a copy (access);
  • have it corrected (rectification) or deleted (erasure);
  • ask us to stop using it (restriction) or to stop contacting you (objection);
  • receive it in a portable format (portability).

Write to g.mascheri@gmail.com. We reply within one month. If you are not satisfied, you may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — for example the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr) or the Italian Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top of this page. Substantial changes affecting enquiries already received will be communicated by email.
